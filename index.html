A GenAI workshop for the Department of Management, LSE

Use GenAI to make learning resources you can keep

Set up a Claude Project with your own course materials, then use it to make interactive activities and animated explainer videos for your students. Everything you make is a file you download and keep.

Start with the digital worksheet

It takes you through the whole session, step by step. Short link: bit.ly/dom261029

Open the worksheet
  1. 1

    Before the session

    Two things to do in advance, so you can start building straight away.

    • Request an LSE Claude account If you see “Access denied” when you open shared resources, follow the steps on Troubleshooting to sign in with SSO.
    • Choose three to five teaching files from one course For example your course outline, one week’s slides and a case study. Leave out anything about individual students.
  2. 2

    In the session

    Keep the worksheet open in one browser tab and Claude in another.

    • Digital worksheet Steps, prompts to copy and examples, in 15 screens.
    • Accessible PDF version of the worksheet The full worksheet as a tagged PDF, for screen readers and other assistive technology, with all the prompts written out.
    • Workshop slides The slides from the session, with the same steps and links as the worksheet.
  3. 3

    After the session

    Optional extras for when you have more time.

    • Go further screens in the worksheet How Claude reads your files, hosting and sharing your resources, creating a style guide, and building and testing.
    • Creating effective Claude Artifacts: a guide for LSE educators Opens in Claude. LSE Claude account needed.
    • AI and Education Toolkit Guides for LSE educators, based on the AI and Education Fellows’ projects. LSE login needed.
    • Visual explainers made with Claude Each topic has an interactive version and a PDF handout.
